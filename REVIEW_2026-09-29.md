# Dashboard safety fixes

Built on upstream frontend 983f8a8. Backend companion changes are required for `/acknowledge-unpaid`.

- Refresh full order list every 30 seconds even when idle.
- Show errors for mutations and align controls with permitted workflow transitions.
- Keep unresolved provider outcomes under manual review; the operator must verify no successful booking/reschedule/payment before acknowledging and retrying.
- Protect controls while a worker owns the order.
- Resume failed requests through READY and eligibility verification.
- Do not show unknown fees as zero or send mutation payloads to Sentry.
- Fix ESLint errors and add CI lint/build checks.

Local build and lint passed. This does not prove deployed frontend/backend compatibility or a live Prometric booking. See backend `docs/SAFETY_REVIEW_2026-09-29.md` for the full review.
